- Major, the shell lexer lost step with sh. Fix: a pre-pass, `risk_sh_prepare()`, rewrites the
  line before the substitution scan and the tokeniser, at any nesting of `$(...)`. An unquoted

  read in order, and the body lines come out of a vectorised line table. A here-string `<<<`
  becomes the marker. `<<` inside `((`/`$((` is a shift. `risk_command()` removes the marker and
  its text from the simple command; when a word of that command is a shell, the text is
  classified as a command line. Typed `\002` bytes are dropped at depth 0. Backstops: the
  tokeniser marks a line that ends inside a quote (attribute `open`), and `risk_command()` adds
  a 3 `dynamic` row for it. A substitution that is not closed inside an unquoted heredoc makes
  the rest of the body data and is 3 `dynamic`. The reviewer's eight scenarios are now 4.
  `git commit -m "$(cat <<'EOF' ... It's ... EOF)"` is 2, and the body is not read as commands.
