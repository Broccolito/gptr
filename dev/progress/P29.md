## Task 11 - k > 1, sequential rounds and the blind judge (2026-10-28)
- `k` from 1 to 5. `strategy = "parallel"` is one round: `k` starting runs, `k` coaches of each focus, a probe of
  each, `k` composers that each see every probe, and `k` final runs. `"sequential"` is `k` rounds over the same
  starting runs, each built on the composition of the round before and shown every earlier round. A call starts
  `k * (2 + focuses)` runs and `k * (1 + focuses)` coach sessions, plus up to one judge session when `k > 1`.
- When more than one final passes, a blind judge picks one: role `judge`, no tools, reply `{label}` from an enum,
  shown only the task and what each passing final delivered, under labels that `coach_shuffle()` orders from a
  seed drawn from the start hash and the prompt, so R's random number generator is untouched. It ranks delivery,
  conformance, fidelity, consistency, fitness and finish. A judge that does not fit or answers no label falls back
  to the first final that passed.
- Record labels `start1`, `proc1`, `dom1`, `focus<j><i>`, `probe-proc1`, `compose1`, `final1` and `judge`; at
  `k > 1` the reason names the final (`final2, picked by the judge: ...`, `final1, the first that passed: ...`).
  A composer keeps only rules from its base or from candidates it was shown, and a plan limit at any stage, the
  judge included, stops the call with the starting harness. Commit eedb4964.
